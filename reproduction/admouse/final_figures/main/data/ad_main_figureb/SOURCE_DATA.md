# AD spatial populations

The spatial panels use the baseline particle states and k=1 cell-type labels
from `admouse_population_data.zip`. The model times 0.4, 1.0 and 1.2 correspond
to the displayed ages of 3.8, 5.7 and 8.1 months.

Download and calculation steps are in the
[AD figure guide](../../../../../../docs/tutorials/paper_figures/admouse_figures.md).
