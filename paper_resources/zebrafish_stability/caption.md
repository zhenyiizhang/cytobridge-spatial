**Supplementary Fig. S40. Stability of the interaction and intrinsic-context decomposition in zebrafish.**

**a,** Agreement among five independently trained default models with training seeds 42, 43, 44, 46, and 47. Each point represents one of the ten pairwise model comparisons on the same 11,999 observed cells at five model times. Total-dynamics and intrinsic-context agreement are median cellwise cosine similarities in the 50-dimensional expression-state space. Growth agreement is the Spearman correlation across cells. Interaction agreement is the Spearman correlation between directed cell-type-pair rankings calculated from exact directed-edge messages. Across the five models, the coefficients of variation of mean spatial, expression-state, and joint Wasserstein-1 distances were 4.11%, 0.90%, and 0.98%, respectively. **b,** Stability after changing one model setting at a time. The left panel shows cellwise cosine similarity of the interaction-associated state component. The right panel shows Spearman rank agreement and weighted overlap among the highest-ranked 20% of active directed cell-type-pair entries. The neighborhood cutoff was set to 0.8 or 1.2 times its default value in three matched training seeds. The expression-loss weight was set to 0.05, and the transport-to-mass weights were set to 10:1 in the seed-42 model. Each model is compared with the default model trained with the same seed. **c,** Percentage change in mean Wasserstein-1 distance from the initial population to observed target times 1--4 after changing one model setting. Each model is compared with the default model trained with the same seed. Lower values indicate better reconstruction. Spatial and expression-state distances are shown separately. The neighbor-independent gene-readout offset of the interaction network is assigned to the intrinsic-context component because it does not depend on neighboring cells. This convention leaves the total model dynamics unchanged.
