**Supplementary Fig. S43. Cross-method comparison of CytoBridge interaction summaries.** (a) Spearman rank correlations compare the complete directed cell-type-pair rankings from CytoBridge with COMMOT and the shared-database CellAgentChat proxy. Jaccard indices compare the corresponding top 20% of directed pairs. (b) Representative ligand--receptor axes selected from the CytoBridge interaction contribution before external comparison. The COMMOT columns report the rank of the same ligand--receptor axis within the selected directed pair and the percentile of that directed pair. (c) For each dataset, the highest-ranked CytoBridge ligand--receptor--directed-pair axis with a matched positive NicheNet ligand--target link is shown together with the same-axis COMMOT percentile and the NicheNet-predicted receiver targets. Zebrafish NicheNet results use a strict one-to-one Ensembl mapping to the mouse prior and are presented as a cross-species sensitivity analysis. These comparisons summarize agreement among methods applied to matched expression and spatial inputs.
