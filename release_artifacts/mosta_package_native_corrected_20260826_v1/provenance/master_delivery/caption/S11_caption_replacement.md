**Supplementary Figure S11. Ligand–receptor temporal patterns in the MOSTA dataset.** Shape-clustered ligand–receptor communication profiles across the corrected MOSTA trajectory. Curves are grouped into three reproducible dynamic patterns using package-native total cell-type communication (`M_sum`). Pattern 1 (orange) is strongest at the earliest stage and declines over time. Pattern 2 (blue) shows a broad early peak followed by a gradual decline. Pattern 3 (green) increases during intermediate-to-late stages and then plateaus or partially declines. Lines are display-only interpolations through the seven computed half-step values, which are shown as markers.
